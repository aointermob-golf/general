About Me

Experience

With over 30 years professional experience I bring a range of different ways of thinking to the change space. There is no single right way to do change so you need a diverse toolkit to make things happen.

Lean Change

Why spend lengthy periods of time developing material that will never be referred to or used once you have put all that effort in.

We want to be productive and effective with our change approach and show the results with positive actions and collaboration.

I Love Tech

I have a passion for tech and what it can do FOR US! There are too many situations (and businesses) using tech ON US or having tech take over things in our lives (what tech does TO US). It is an enabler and should always allow you to feel like you are in control.

Products I'm Passionate About

From the moment I started using Salesforce I could see the potential in how it could help businesses and individuals. I'm also evenly divided between the world of PC (Microsoft 365 or Office 365) and the world of Apple. Everything has its pros and cons and I like to think I'm adaptable between these worlds.

Don't Stand Still!

I am not asking you to continually move but at least be a little adventurous and sometimes step into an area that makes you feel uncomfortable and give it a try. Life is constantly changing around us and sometimes we just need to give things a go.

"If you could do something better, should you?"

I would hope that your answer is yes to this and if you need a little help then that is where Change Management might just be the answer.
