**AGENTS OF CHANGE**

So change is this thing that we all do,  
With tools and frameworks that we all plough through!  
Whose methodology should we use?  
Is it ADKAR or Kotter or some other brew!

Lean or Heavy or some hybrid mix?  
Where do I start and what do I fix?  
Some say it's easy and some are confused  
But there is one thing to keep us amused!

Change is apparently something that's tough  
And the road for all is one that is rough!  
But we all go through change in our day to day lives  
So what makes our jobs such a hard strive?

Everyone's journey is unique and special  
So we need to be trusted to help ease the wrestle  
So how do we get the 'stakeholders' on board?  
We listen, acknowledge, develop rapport!

The journey of change is not about us,  
We are simply there to help drive the bus!  
Now, I don't want you to think it's a walk in the park,  
It takes the skill of a ninja to make a positive mark!

So be kind, attentive, and never give in,  
As we continue with change and helping all win!

*Andrew O'Shea — 24/11/2022*
