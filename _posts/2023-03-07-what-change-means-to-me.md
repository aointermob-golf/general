I decided a video (VLOG) was going to be better this time around to share my personal feelings about what change management means to me. There will be some that think what I am saying is obvious, and others that may completely disagree. This is the beauty of Change Management and all the various ways it can be approached.

The video is 10 minutes and I hope you make it to the end. There is no background music or noise, just me being me (the backdrop is what will be my backyard when we move). It is also the first time I have been comfortable looking down the camera and I did the take in one sitting with no breaks and no notes. This one really did come from somewhere deep inside.

Thanks for watching (if you did). 🙏🏻
